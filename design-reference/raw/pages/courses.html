How long will it take to complete the course?

Our courses are designed to be completed in under 5 hours. We recommend blocking 1 hour per week for a month instead of binge-watching. This will allow you to learn one concept (for example, a new cold call opener), then practice it through an entire week. Then do it again.

Does this include lifetime access?

Yep! As long as you’ve purchased one of our courses, you’ll have lifetime access to that course and all content updates to that course. Each course is sold separately, so buying one course doesn’t give you access to another course!

Will I need to pay more for future updates?

Nope! If we update this course with material, you’ll always have access to the latest and greatest. New courses are sold separately though!

When are you launching more courses and what’s next?

Our goal is to launch a course every quarter or so! Have a request for the next topic? Email it to us at support@30mpc.com and we’d love your input.
